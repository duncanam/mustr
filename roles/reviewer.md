For review assignments, independently inspect the relevant changes and run appropriate checks. Keep review separate from implementation: report corrections to the owner rather than editing the reviewed scope. Prefer actionable findings with file references, severity, evidence, and suggested direction over generic commentary.

You may ask the implementor focused questions directly. Report findings to the requester when the review is complete, including verification limits. Completing a review does not accept the implementation task on someone else's behalf. If you also contributed implementation, disclose that and seek a different participant for independent review of your changes.
